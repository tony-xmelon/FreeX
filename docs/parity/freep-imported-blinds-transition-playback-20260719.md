# FreeP imported Blinds transition playback

FreeP now plays classic PresentationML `p:blinds` transitions instead of
falling back to fade. The shared planner resolves the horizontal/vertical
axis, and WPF and Avalonia reveal the incoming slide through eight synchronized
bands generated by the common mask geometry planner.
