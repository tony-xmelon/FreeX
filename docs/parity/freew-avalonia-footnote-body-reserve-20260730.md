# Avalonia Footnote Body Reserve

## Scope

The imported `f2-footnotes.docx` fixture uses short footnotes on two ordinary
body pages. Word reserves a small amount of body-frame clearance above each
painted footnote band. Avalonia previously reserved only the painted band,
leaving one additional body paragraph on page 1 and shifting page 2 upward.

The correction is local to Avalonia footnote-body reflow. It adds 24 DIPs to
the body reservation after the shared note fragment is measured; it does not
change note paint geometry or endnote layout.

## Matching Evidence

All images use the fresh 816 x 1056 Word COM reference generated by
`Run-FreeWVisualEvidence.ps1` on 2026-07-30. The Word automation trace records
an isolated visible Word instance becoming ready, opening the fixture,
exporting a short staged PDF, closing the document, and quitting the owned
process.

| Fixture page | Avalonia before | Avalonia after | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `f2-footnotes` p1 | 7.7086% | 2.8044% | -4.9042 pp |
| `f2-footnotes` p2 | 4.8729% | 1.8232% | -3.0497 pp |

The body-page sequence now matches Word: `More filler 1` remains on page 1,
and `More filler 2` starts page 2. The two `f2-endnotes` candidate PNGs are
SHA-256 identical to the pre-change Avalonia captures, confirming that the
footnote-only reserve does not alter endnote composition.
