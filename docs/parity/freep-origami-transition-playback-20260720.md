# FreeP Origami transition playback

Origami now routes through the shared double-fold page projection. The route
preserves the paper-fold silhouette, direction, and two-sided fold timing in
WPF and Avalonia instead of reducing Origami to a fade. It is a bounded 2-D
projection and does not claim a full multi-plane origami simulation.

Planner and Page Curl geometry contracts cover the source mapping and double
fold selection. No PowerPoint COM raster export was issued for this
function-focused slice.
