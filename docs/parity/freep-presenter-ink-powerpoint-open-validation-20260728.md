# FreeP Presenter Ink PowerPoint Validation - 2026-07-28

## Scope

Validate that presenter ink generated by the shared `SlideShowInkPersistencePlanner` is a native PowerPoint Ink Content Part, not only a FreeP-preserved XML payload.

## Fix

Generated ink now follows the native Office contract:

- the slide relationship uses the standard `customXml` relationship type;
- the InkML part declares `application/inkml+xml`;
- the slide `p:contentPart` uses the PowerPoint 2010 `p14` non-visual properties and transform;
- InkML includes `definitions`, `context`, `inkSource`, `traceFormat`, and per-stroke `brush` definitions;
- each trace carries `contextRef` and its own `brushRef`, preserving color, thickness, opacity, and highlighter semantics;
- the content-part transform is bounded to the authored ink extents instead of the whole slide;
- the reader accepts both the older `p` namespace spelling and native `p14` content-part properties by local element name.

The relationship and InkML requirements follow Microsoft's [Ink Content Part specification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/office_standards/ms-odrawxml/096dacae-0d2c-4861-bc4d-c8e4c6405ad3) and [PresentationML content-part example](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/office_standards/ms-odrawxml/a1a880a0-1d58-4a39-80c8-6f87161671fb).

## Verification

The probe invoked the actual shared planner with one pen stroke, wrote a real `.pptx`, and passed it to the installed PowerPoint COM exporter:

```text
dotnet tools/FreeP.RenderCompare/bin/Release/net10.0-windows10.0.19041.0/FreeP.RenderCompare.dll --generate-presenter-ink-probe <output.pptx>
dotnet tools/FreeP.RenderCompare/bin/Release/net10.0-windows10.0.19041.0/FreeP.RenderCompare.dll --powerpoint-export <output.pptx> <export-dir> --width 1280 --height 720
```
