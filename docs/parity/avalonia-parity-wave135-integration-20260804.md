# Avalonia Parity Wave 135 Integration

Wave 135 advances one bounded parity slice in each app without weakening the
existing functional contracts.

## FreeX

`dialog.AllowEditRanges` now uses the WPF-shaped visible layout while retaining
range pointing through the shared dialog range picker. The picker remains hidden
from the permanent dialog chrome; F4 enters point mode, and Enter applies the
pointed selection and restores the dialog.

The refreshed paired evidence reduced the triage score from `0.078735` to
`0.022747`, sample mean delta from `0.022148` to `0.015483`, luminance delta from
`0.013911` to `0.003944`, and non-background delta from `0.042398` to `0.003040`.
The global manifest remains complete at 94 WPF and 94 Avalonia surfaces, with 94
pairs and no nonblank, logical-dimension, or expected-size failures. The highest
remaining FreeX triage score is `0.077246` (`dialog.About`).

## FreeW

`borders-and-shading` now preserves the constructor-seeded edge checkbox state
used by the WPF route capture, while the real modal route still applies the
paragraph setting plan before display. The selected tab pane also uses the shared
classic-tab chrome compensation so its content frame aligns more closely with
WPF.

Fresh WPF captures paired with the pane-corrected Avalonia captures reduced the
initial and populated changed-pixel ratio from `11.2824%` to `11.1902%` and mean
channel delta from `6.5772` to `6.4982`. The validation state moved from
`11.3857%` to `11.2935%` and from `6.7362` to `6.6573`. These rows remain genuine
visual mismatches and are not claimed as full visual parity.

## FreeP

The shared SmartArt import planner now admits a strict
`increasingCircleProcess` cache: four ordered, distinct, nonempty growing ellipse
nodes and three empty line connectors. WPF and Avalonia consume the same shared
geometry. Malformed, richer, effectful, picture-bearing, or otherwise out-of-
grammar caches continue through the existing cached fallback path.

The deterministic render corpus grows from 8 to 9 slides, and generated workflow
evidence grows from 106 to 107 rows. This is WPF/Avalonia app-owned parity
evidence; it does not claim PowerPoint-identical rendering.

## Boundary

This wave improves three measured slices. It does not declare whole-product
Avalonia parity: remaining visual mismatches, deeper workflow families, and
authoritative Microsoft Office baselines continue to be tracked separately by
the generated parity inventories and dashboard.
